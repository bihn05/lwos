Licence
-------
Per the "Copyright and license" section of that page, the official GPL, AGPL
and LGPL logos are in the public domain, and the page says outright:

    If you are releasing work under ... version 3 of the GNU GPL, LGPL, or
    AGPL, feel free to use these buttons on your site or in your application
    to advertise the license.

Public domain means there is no restriction on modifying them either, so a
recoloured or pixelated variant would need no permission. The design credit
above is a courtesy, not a condition.

How it is used
--------------
Byte for byte — no recolouring, no re-encoding, no pixelation. It sits in the
banner's bottom row, right beside bochs. That part of the page is 30px Arial
Black text and gradient pills, i.e. high resolution, so the anti-aliased
original belongs there. A pixelated variant was tried and dropped: next to
bochs. it just looked mushy. The hand-drawn 88x31 GIFs further down the page
are a different, deliberately low-res collage.

Deliberately the plain GPLv3 button, NOT gplv3-or-later-sm.png from the same
page: that one reads "OR LATER" and would misstate GPL-3.0-only.

It keeps an alpha channel, so the background shows through the corners — that
is how the original is built. The badge sits on the banner, which is white.
