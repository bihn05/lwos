Licence
-------
Per the "Copyright and license" section of that page, the official GPL, AGPL
and LGPL logos are in the public domain, and the page says outright:

    If you are releasing work under ... version 3 of the GNU GPL, LGPL, or
    AGPL, feel free to use these buttons on your site or in your application
    to advertise the license.

Public domain means there is no restriction on modifying them, so the cropping
below needs no permission. The design credit above is a courtesy, not a
condition.

What was changed, and only this
-------------------------------
Upstream is an Inkscape A4 page (744.09 x 1052.36, no viewBox at all) holding
TWO lockups — a red one and a black duplicate — plus a "Free as in Freedom"
line, so it cannot be used as-is:

  * dropped <g id="g3413">          the whole black duplicate lockup (-40372 B)
  * dropped <g id="g2587..g2643>    the 15 glyph groups of "Free as in Freedom"
  * replaced the root width/height  with a tight viewBox around the badge:
                                    "36.66 19.16 698.86 279.25"

82482 -> 30816 bytes. Everything else — the Inkscape metadata, defs, namedview,
the red group's transform — is byte for byte upstream. The four remaining paths
are path2577/path2579/path2583 (flat #bd0000, no stroke) and path2647 (the
swoosh, stroke-width 8.53, so the box is padded by half of that). The box also
counts Bezier control points, i.e. it is conservative: nothing gets clipped.

Framing, and why the size is 78x31
----------------------------------
The box is the complete badge at aspect 2.5026, which matches the official
127x51 raster (2.490) to within 0.7%. The official 88x31 button is NOT this
framing (2.839) — it is a wider, shorter crop of the same artwork — so do not
expect the two to overlay. index.astro renders the badge at a height of 31px to
match the row it sits in, which makes it 77.58px wide, hence width="78".

Deliberately the plain GPLv3 badge, NOT the "or later" one from the same page:
gplv3-or-later.svg reads "OR LATER" and would misstate GPL-3.0-only.
